Why the cap fixes the percentage

Where the cap governs, holders own amount ÷ cap of everything except the new money — later instruments and the BSPCE increase dilute the founders, not them. Only the round itself dilutes.

What the floor is for

Below the floor pivot the warrants convert at the floor whatever the round pays. It bounds investor ownership in a down round, which is what keeps the band intact at the low end — and it is why the curve turns twice rather than once.

The trade-off to disclose

In a hard down round the floor can push the warrant price above the round price, so the earlier investor pays more per share than the later one. Watch for the amber flag above the capitalization table.

Indicative model, not legal, tax or investment advice. The BSA-AIR is a French-law instrument: it carries a long-stop date, it grants no claim on a dissolution, and it offers no cash-out branch on a liquidity event. Confirm every figure against Hywave's actual capitalization table, and the structure with French counsel, before use.
